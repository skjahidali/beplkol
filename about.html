About BEPL

Engineering with
purpose and precision.

Bewandert Engineers Private Limited (BEPL) is an engineering solutions firm headquartered in West Bengal, delivering multidisciplinary civil, industrial and project execution services.

Who we are

Emerging. Dynamic. Precise.

Founded in 2023, BEPL works across civil infrastructure, structural engineering, technical consultancy, industrial projects, specialist testing and analysis, and end-to-end project management. We coordinate the project lifecycle from planning and design through site execution and final handover, with a focus on safety, quality, resource use and clear communication.
